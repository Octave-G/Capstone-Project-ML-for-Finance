# Machine Learning in Finance - Capstone Project

This project was made for the FALL 2025 course PBL4005, Machine Learning in Finance, taught by Professor Sang Bin Lee (이상빈). The capstone project directive of this course was to find a potential area within finance where we could apply machine learning models.

At this time, I was also taking the course ECO2061 on Time Series Modelling and Forecasting, taught by Professor Han Hyojin (한효진). I gained a deep interest in this subject, and I wanted to find a common area where I could employ and compare both methodologies. However, since the course focused primarily on Machine Learning methods, the Time Series part was condensed.

I built two predictive models, a Random Forest model and an AutoRegressive Distributed Lag (ARDL) model, to analyze and forecast the U.S. 10-Year Treasury Bond Yield.

For my exogenous variables, I took a mix of financial assets such as Gold, Oil, and FX rates. Some major economic indicators: GDP, Unemployment rate, and the VIX index; and lastly, Treasury bonds with different maturities.

You can look at the Random Forest model outputs and explanation in the files: **RandomForest_Output.ipynb** and **RandomForest_Explanation.pdf**.
Similarly, for the ARDL model, see the files: **ARDL_Output.ipynb** and **ARDL_Explanation.pdf**.
